Build a sales / operations dashboard app.

A level-1 heading and a small muted subtitle, then a **stat grid** of three KPI tiles
(revenue as currency with an up delta, orders, average order as currency), a **line chart**
of revenue by month (x = month, y = revenue), and a **data table** of the monthly records
(columns month, revenue, orders — sortable, with totals). Seed a `months` variable as a list
of monthly records (fields: month, revenue, orders). Light theme, blue accent.

Components: heading · text · statgrid (stat KPI tiles) · chart (line, reads months) · datatable (readVar months).
